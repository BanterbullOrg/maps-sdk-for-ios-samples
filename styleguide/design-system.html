06 — Components

The catalogue

Masthead

.masthead — sticky 64px glass bar, blur(16px), hairline bottom. Nav hides ≤900px.

Hero

.hero — two columns: copy + a 540px live market card. Mono eyebrow with a glowing accent dot.

Steps

.steps — 4-up hairline grid, radius-lg, bg-1 tiles; mono accent numbers.

Why cards

.card — bg-1 + hairline, 24px accent icon; hover reveals a soft radial accent glow.

Stat strip

.stats — 3-up; 56px light figures with an accent emphasis span.

Closing CTA

.close-cta — centred, blurred radial accent glow, centred waitlist.

Market card

.mcard — binary market with inline-SVG accent chart, dashed grid, animated end ping, YES/NO buttons.

Board

.mqcol / .bc — auto-scrolling column of mini market cards (30s marquee, pauses on hover).

Consent

.consent — floating glass pill. Production uses the region-aware widget injected site-wide by the Worker.

Footer

4-column grid + a mono legal row (year + legal links).
